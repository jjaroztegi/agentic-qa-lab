# CP-004 S355J2 sales order reaches the MES

## Application Overview

ERP (Odoo, plays SAP) receives the sales order and, through the MTO + manufacture route, creates a
manufacturing order. The in-house MES picks the order up from the ERP (sync every ~10 s) and lists it
as a pending order with customer, steel grade and planned tonnes.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. S355J2 sales order reaches the MES with the correct customer, grade and quantity

**Seed:** `tests/e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1 CP-004 S355J2 sales order reaches the MES with the correct customer, grade and quantity

**File:** `tests/e2e/cp-004-s355j2-order-reaches-mes.spec.ts`

**Steps:**
1. In the ERP, as the sales rep, create a quotation for the customer with the product and quantity, and confirm it
   - Sales → New
   - Customer: "Meridian Construction Inc."
   - Line: product "Hot-rolled coil S355J2", quantity 25.0 (t)
   - Confirm
   - expect: the quotation becomes a sales order (status "Sales Order") with number `S0xxxx`
2. Check that the ERP creates a manufacturing order for the sales order
   - expect: a manufacturing order exists with origin = the sales order number, 25.0 t of "Hot-rolled coil S355J2" (ERP data; the sales rep has no Manufacturing access)
3. In the MES, open the list of manufacturing orders
   - expect: the MES "Manufacturing orders" list is shown
4. Find the order that arrives from the ERP for that sales order
   - expect: the sales order's row shows customer "Meridian Construction Inc."
   - expect: the order shows grade "S355J2"
   - expect: the order shows a quantity of 25.000 t
   - expect: the order has status "Pending"

**Data checks:**
- ERP `sale.order` (the created sales order): state = sale, customer = Meridian Construction Inc.
- ERP `mrp.production` (origin = sales order): product_qty = 25.0, product Hot-rolled coil S355J2
- MES `GET /api/orders/{ERP order}`: customer = Meridian Construction Inc., grade = S355J2, qty_t = 25.0, status = pending
