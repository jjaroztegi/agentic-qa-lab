# CP-009 The MES rejects a coil with a malformed heat number

## Application Overview

The in-house rolling-mill MES (tested here as a black box) lists manufacturing orders received from the
ERP. On an order's screen the shift supervisor registers produced coils (coil no., heat no., weight).
Heat numbers have 6 digits; the MES must refuse a coil whose heat number does not.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. The MES rejects a coil with a malformed heat number

**Seed:** `tests/e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1 CP-009 The MES rejects a coil with a malformed heat number

**File:** `tests/e2e/cp-009-mes-rejects-malformed-heat.spec.ts`

**Precondition:** a pending or in-progress order exists in the MES (taken from `GET /api/orders`, e.g. `WH/MO/00001`).

**Steps:**
1. Open the order in the MES
   - Go to the MES order list and click the order's link (e.g. `WH/MO/00001`)
   - expect: the order screen is shown with its coil registration form
2. Try to add a coil with heat no. 52340
   - Coil no.: `C-<RUN>-1`, heat no.: `52340`, weight: 22000 kg
   - Thickness 3 mm, width 1500 mm (the form requires them; the case does not give them)
   - Register coil
   - expect: the MES shows "Heat no. must have 6 digits" and does not register the coil (coil `C-<RUN>-1` not listed)

**Data checks:**
- MES `GET /api/orders/{order}`: the number of coils does not change (same as before step 2), and no coil `C-<RUN>-1` exists
