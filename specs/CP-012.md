# CP-012 Maintenance · Breakdown report

## Application Overview

ERP (Odoo 19) Maintenance app. The shift supervisor (login `shift.supervisor`) reports a mill breakdown
as a maintenance request. The equipment "Finishing mill · Stand F3" is master data, assigned to the
maintenance team "Rolling mill maintenance".

## Test Scenarios

### 1. A mill breakdown reported by the shift supervisor reaches maintenance as an urgent corrective request

**Seed:** `tests/e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1 CP-012 A mill breakdown reported by the shift supervisor reaches maintenance as an urgent corrective request

**File:** `tests/e2e/cp-012-breakdown-urgent-corrective-request.spec.ts`

**Steps:**
  1. Log into the ERP as the shift supervisor (`shift.supervisor`).
  2. In the ERP, as the shift supervisor, open Maintenance and create a request with the subject "Abnormal vibration on stand F3" for the equipment "Finishing mill · Stand F3".
    - expect: the request form opens; the maintenance team is filled in by the ERP from the equipment (the supervisor does not choose it).
  3. Set type Corrective and the highest priority (3 stars), and save.
    - expect: the request is saved.
    - expect: the request is in the stage "New Request".
    - expect: the maintenance team is "Rolling mill maintenance".
    - expect: the requester (Created by) is the shift supervisor.
  4. Data validation (ERP API): the `maintenance.request` with that subject has maintenance_type = corrective, priority = 3, stage_id = New Request, maintenance_team_id = Rolling mill maintenance, owner_user_id = shift supervisor.
