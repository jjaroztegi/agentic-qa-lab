# CP-007 Order-to-coil with an L2 measurement hold

## Application Overview

ERP (Odoo, plays SAP) receives the sales order and, through the MTO + manufacture route, creates a
manufacturing order. The in-house MES picks the order up from the ERP (sync every ~10 s) and records
the rolled coils. The Level 2 client (vendor Java desktop app) registers the line-exit measurement of
a coil; the MES checks it against the grade specification (S355J2: thickness ± 0.12 mm, width −0/+10 mm,
flatness ≤ 12 I) and holds the coil automatically when it is out of tolerance. On completion the MES
reports the produced tonnes (released coils only) back to the ERP.

### 1. From the ERP sales order to confirmed production, with a coil held by its Level 2 measurement

#### 1.1 CP-007 From the ERP sales order to confirmed production, with a coil held by its Level 2 measurement

**File:** `tests/e2e/cp-007-order-to-coil-l2-measurement-hold.spec.ts`

**Steps:**
1. In the ERP, as the sales rep, create and confirm a sales order for 48 t of "Hot-rolled coil S355J2" for "Meridian Construction Inc."
   - Sales → New; Customer "Meridian Construction Inc."; Add a product "Hot-rolled coil S355J2", quantity 48; Confirm
   - expect: status "Sales Order", number `S0xxxx`; a manufacturing order with origin = that sales order exists (ERP data)
2. In the MES, open the order that arrives from the ERP and add both coils as released
   - expect: the MES "Manufacturing orders" list shows the order with customer "Meridian Construction Inc.", grade S355J2 and 48 t
   - Register `C-<RUN>-1`: heat 523401, 24,100 kg, 4.0 mm × 1500 mm, Released
   - Register `C-<RUN>-2`: heat 523401, 23,650 kg, 4.0 mm × 1500 mm, Released
   - expect: both coils appear on the order as Released
3. In the Level 2 client, press "Measurements", load coil 2 and register thickness 4.18, width 1503 and flatness 7
   - Window "Level 2 · Hot strip mill"; tab "Measurements"; "Coil to measure" = `C-<RUN>-2`; "Load coil";
     measured thickness 4.18, width 1503, flatness 7; "Register measurement"
   - expect: the warning "Measurement out of tolerance" is shown
4. In the Level 2 client, press "Coils" and check the status of coil 2
   - expect: coil 2 is HOLD with reason "Thickness out of tolerance", without the operator pressing "Hold"
5. In the MES, complete the order and confirm production to the ERP
   - expect: the MES shows produced "24.100 t" (only the released coil counts) and status "Confirmed in ERP"
   - expect: in the ERP the manufacturing order is done with 24.1 t produced

**Data checks:**
- MES `GET /api/coils/{id of coil 2}`: quality = HOLD, hold_motive = Thickness out of tolerance
- MES `GET /api/orders/{order}`: produced_t = 24.1, status = reported
- ERP `mrp.production` (origin = sales order): state = done, qty_produced = 24.1
