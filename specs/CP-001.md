# CP-001 Order-to-coil

## Application Overview

ERP (Odoo, plays SAP) receives the sales order and, through the MTO + manufacture route, creates a
manufacturing order. The in-house MES picks the order up from the ERP (sync every ~10 s), records the
rolled coils and, on completion, reports the produced tonnes (released coils only) back to the ERP.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Hot-rolled coil sales order through to production confirmed in the ERP

**Seed:** `tests/e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1 CP-001 Hot-rolled coil sales order through to production confirmed in the ERP

**File:** `tests/e2e/cp-001-order-to-coil.spec.ts`

**Steps:**
1. In the ERP, as the sales rep, create a quotation for the customer with the product and quantity, and confirm it
   - Sales → Orders → Quotations → New
   - Customer: "Northline Fabrication Ltd."
   - Line: product "Hot-rolled coil S275JR", quantity 48.5 (t)
   - Confirm
   - expect: the quotation becomes a sales order (status "Sales Order") with number `S0xxxx`
2. Check that the ERP creates a manufacturing order for the sales order
   - expect: a manufacturing order exists with origin = the sales order number ("Manufacturing" smart button on the order, or ERP data)
3. In the MES, open the order that arrives from the ERP for that sales order
   - expect: the MES "Manufacturing orders" list shows an order referencing the sales order; opening it shows customer, product and 48.500 t planned
4. Register the three coils with their data
   - `C-<RUN>-1`: heat 512345, 24,500 kg, 3.5 mm × 1500 mm, quality status Released
   - `C-<RUN>-2`: heat 512345, 23,250 kg, 3.5 mm × 1500 mm, quality status Released
   - `C-<RUN>-3`: heat 512346, 22,000 kg, 3.5 mm × 1500 mm, quality status Held
   - expect: the three coils appear on the order
5. Complete the order
   - expect: the MES order changes to "Confirmed in ERP"
   - expect: the MES shows produced 47.750 t (released coils only)
   - expect: in the ERP the manufacturing order is done with 47.750 t produced

**Data checks:**
- ERP `mrp.production` (origin = sales order): state = done, qty_produced = 47.75
- MES `GET /api/orders/{order}`: status = reported, produced_t = 47.75
