# CP-005 A sales order with a committed delivery date reaches the MES with that date

## Application Overview

ERP (Odoo, plays SAP) receives the sales order with a committed delivery date ("Delivery Date" in the
Other Info tab). Through the MTO + manufacture route, confirming it creates a manufacturing order. The
in-house MES picks the order up from the ERP (sync every ~10 s) and lists it with customer, grade,
planned tonnes and due date, which mill sequencing uses.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. A sales order with a committed delivery date reaches the MES with that date

**Seed:** `tests/e2e/seed.spec.ts`

#### 1.1 CP-005 A sales order with a committed delivery date reaches the MES with that date

**File:** `tests/e2e/cp-005-committed-date-reaches-mes.spec.ts`

**Steps:**
1. In the ERP, as the sales rep, create a quotation for the customer with the product and quantity, and set the committed delivery date
   - Sales → New
   - Customer: "Atlantic Tube Ltd."
   - Line: product "Pickled coil DD11", quantity 30 (t)
   - Other Info tab → Delivery Date: 2026-12-15
2. Confirm the sales order in the ERP
   - expect: the quotation becomes a sales order (status "Sales Order") with number `S0xxxx`
   - expect: ERP `sale.order` state = sale, commitment_date = 2026-12-15
3. The ERP creates the manufacturing order and the MES picks it up automatically (interface every 10 s); nobody acts in between
4. In the MES, open the list of manufacturing orders
   - expect: the MES "Manufacturing orders" list is shown
5. Find the order that corresponds to the sales order
   - expect: the MES list shows an order for the sales order with customer "Atlantic Tube Ltd."
   - expect: that order shows grade "DD11"
   - expect: that order shows quantity 30 t (30.000)
   - expect: that order shows due date 2026-12-15, equal to the committed delivery date in the ERP
   - expect: the order appears in the MES within 1 minute of the confirmation

**Data checks:**
- ERP `sale.order`: state = sale, commitment_date = 2026-12-15
- MES `GET /api/orders` (order whose sale_ref is the sales order): customer = Atlantic Tube Ltd., grade = DD11, qty_t = 30, due_date = 2026-12-15
